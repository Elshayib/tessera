## What to build

An installed Windows app with a live Viewport. The Person watches Clay appear as Verbs land. Optional Orbit: the Agent does not fight an Orbit in progress, then Frames again on the next Verb. One Scene on screen. No account.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Person can install/run Tessera on Windows with no account.
- [ ] The Viewport shows Clay updating as the First take lands (not a spinner then a result).
- [ ] Chat and Narration are visible beside the Viewport; the Viewport is the product.
- [ ] The Person may Orbit; during Orbit the Agent does not steal the camera; the next Verb Frames again.
- [ ] The core loop works if they never Orbit.
- [ ] One Scene on screen.
- [ ] Product tests still do not have to open a real window; a recording View remains the test adapter.
