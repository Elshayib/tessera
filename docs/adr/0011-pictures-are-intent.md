# Pictures are Intent, not a generator

The Person may drop one or more pictures (silhouette, paint, time of day) alongside words. Those pictures are Intent. The Agent still Composes and Sculpts in Clay. A picture is never a back door into a third-party image-to-3D generator — that would break ADR-0005.

Words-only was rejected: "no skill" includes "I know it when I see it," and a beginner who cannot describe form would be stuck.
