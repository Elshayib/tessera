# Compose from Primitives plus a small Kit

v1 Compose uses a handful of Primitives (box, sphere, cylinder, capsule, cone, torus) and a small named Kit in three families: architecture, nature, props. Enough that "lighthouse on a cliff" is made of lighthouse-shaped pieces, not twelve cylinders.

Primitives-only was rejected: the First take would look like homework and fail the judgeable bar. A large authored Kit as the product was rejected: that is a content studio, and the Agent is only as good as yesterday's pack. A later Verb, Keep as Part, may save a composed-and-sculpted Object as a Part, Scene-local first — not a global junk drawer in v1.
