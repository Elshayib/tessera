# First take must be judgeable; then Rehearsal

The conversation is Rehearsal on one Scene, but the First take has to already be a readable Scene. One-shot (new Scene every request) was rejected: that is an image-to-3D site's loop and throws away watching-and-talking. Rehearsal with no First-take bar was rejected: "no skill" on the brochure becomes "please art-direct a blob" in the product.

The Agent Frames on every Verb so the Person is looking at the work. The Person may Orbit at any time. The Agent does not fight an Orbit in progress; it Frames again on the next Verb. An Agent-locked camera was rejected because judging the other side is part of the loop. A Person-locked camera was rejected because a beginner will stare at the back of the lighthouse for the whole take.
