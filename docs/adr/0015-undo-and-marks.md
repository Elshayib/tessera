# Undo Verbs; restore Marks

The Person can Undo the last Verb (and stack that). They can also Mark a state they liked and restore it. The First take is always a Mark.

No-undo was rejected: Steer and Stop only change what happens next, and Clay you cannot rewind makes the Person afraid to talk. Undo-only was rejected: it cannot bring back a whole earlier take. Marking a take they liked is the beginner form of version control; they should not have to know that word.
