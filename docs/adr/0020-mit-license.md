# Tessera is MIT licensed

Tessera — Engine, Verbs, app, and Kit — is open source under MIT. Closed-source v1 was assumed because the Engine is the product; that was wrong. MIT matches the free app, the Person owning their Work, and the long-run bet that the product is the Agent and the watching loop, not a locked binary.

Consequence: v1 Kit pieces must themselves be MIT-clearable. Third-party packs with a different license do not ship in the Kit.
