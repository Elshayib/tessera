# v1 delivers a Turntable; Game-ready is the destination

"Stunning 3D for games, movies, or anything" is the ambition. v1 is a Turntable: a short orbit of a real 3D scene the Person can judge, with no topology, UV, or rig contract. Game-ready export is the declared destination, not the v1 geometry contract.

A Game-ready-in-v1 path was rejected because an Engine that must emit production topology while also being invented would die before anyone watches anything. A stills-only path was rejected because the product is 3D you watch, not a picture generator. Movie-quality animation is out of reach for v1.
