# The v1 app is free

v1 Tessera-the-app costs nothing. The bill the Person already has is the Key to their Provider. Charging for the app on day one was rejected: that is paying Tessera *and* a Provider, which fights "ready to use." Holding the Scene or a Still hostage behind a paywall was rejected: that is how people hate a tool.

A paid Tessera belongs with a later Tessera-hosted brain (OAuth, no Key paste) — then they pay Tessera instead of a Provider, not both. (a) can become that later. (c) cannot be undone.
