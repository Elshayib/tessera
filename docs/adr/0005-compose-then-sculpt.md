# Form is composed, then sculpted; no third-party generator

The Agent first Composes a readable structure, then Sculpts the silhouette. A third-party neural generator as the source of form was rejected: quality would belong to someone else's weights, which is the Blender-wrapper problem with a different interior. Training our own generator is a research lab, not v1. Pure sculpt from a blob was rejected because a beginner cannot art-direct a blob. Pure compose was rejected because a weathered cliff has to look grown, not only assembled.

v1 "stunning" is a distinctive Tessera look, not a photogrammetry scan. Game-ready later means this representation can become a mesh, not that v1 topology is clean.
